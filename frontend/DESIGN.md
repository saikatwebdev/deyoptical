## Brand & Style

The visual identity is anchored in clinical precision softened by contemporary editorial lifestyle aesthetics. Designed for individuals seeking both prescriptive rigor and modern eyewear curation, the interface projects quiet authority, pristine cleanliness, and understated luxury. 

The aesthetic marries clinical minimalism with modern boutique tactile qualities. Whitespace serves as an active compositional tool rather than empty void, echoing the sterile focus of an optometrist's studio paired with the warmth of a luxury design house. Visual noise is systematically reduced: high-contrast geometric typography, crisp hairline structural borders, and purposeful flashes of clinical teal deliver absolute clarity and immediate medical credibility.

## Colors

The palette leverages a calibrated light-mode hierarchy engineered to communicate medical hygiene and optical clarity:

- **Primary (`#0D9488`)**: A controlled clinical teal reserved for critical user actions, optical status indicators, verified prescription indicators, and primary micro-interactions.
- **Secondary (`#1F2937`)**: Deep mineral slate charcoal, grounding high-priority calls to action, lens selection headers, and structural framing.
- **Tertiary (`#0F766E`)**: A deep sea-teal anchor utilized for hovered interaction states and high-emphasis focus rings.
- **Neutrals & Surfaces**: Built from pure light values (`#FFFFFF` surface base), softly tinted grey backgrounds (`#F8F9FA` canvas, `#F1F3F5` container fills), bounded by subtle architectural hairline borders (`#E5E7EB`). 
- **Typography Neutrals**: Near-black (`#111827`) for authoritative headlines and `#374151` for clinical body text ensure effortless readability.

## Typography

Typography is set exclusively in Inter to achieve an uncompromising balance of neutral modernist functionalism and high legibility. 

Tight negative tracking is applied to display weights (`-0.03em`) to mimic high-end architectural and fashion editorial layouts while maintaining crisp scanning. Body copy sits at generous line-heights (1.6x) to reduce optical fatigue during prescription reviews and diagnostic reads. Micro-labels and optical parameters (pupillary distance, sphere, cylinder, axis) use tabular numbers and uppercase tracking (`+0.04em`) to establish visual order and scientific discipline.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8pt base grid with a fluid 12-column system on desktop and a responsive 4-column flow on mobile viewports.

- **Breakpoints**: Mobile (up to `640px`), Tablet (`641px - 1024px`), Desktop (`1025px+`). Maximum content container width is capped at `1280px` to maintain optimal viewing fields for eyewear photography.
- **Rhythm**: Component interiors prioritize asymmetric vertical-to-horizontal padding ratios (e.g., `space-md` vertical with `space-lg` horizontal) to grant optical cards and interactive controls an expansive, premium presence.
- **Mobile First Adaptation**: Side navigation collates into bottom sheets and smooth gestures on mobile, preserving thumb zones. Grid gutters compress gracefully from `1.5rem` on desktop to `1rem` on mobile, while margins pull back to `1rem` to maximize screen real estate.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects exaggerated drop shadows in favor of a layered clinical architectural structure:

- **Border Hierarchy**: Flat, 1px low-contrast hairline borders (`#E5E7EB`) outline elements on white and grey surfaces, mimicking the fine physical wireframes of titanium spectacles.
- **Ambient Elevation**: Interactive overlays, floating prescription trays, and cart summary sheets utilize subtle, diffused shadows tinted with cool slate: `0 8px 30px -4px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.02)`.
- **Tonal Stacking**: Depth is achieved naturally through surface tone shifts: Level 0 base (`#F8F9FA`), Level 1 cards/drawers (`#FFFFFF`), Level 2 input containers (`#F1F3F5`).
- **Glass & Frost**: Fixed headers, filter bars, and modal backdrops utilize a high-translucency frost (`background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85); backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) to keep optical products subtly visible as users scroll.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a refined standard corner radius (`roundedness: 2`, where base components sit at `0.5rem` / `8px`):

- **Base Components (`0.5rem` / `8px`)**: Buttons, inputs, standard cards, and diagnostic tables maintain balanced, rounded geometric edges that avoid appearing childish while eliminating industrial severity.
- **Containers & Sheets (`1rem` / `16px`)**: Product display tiles, prescription preview modals, and mobile bottom sheet tops scale to `1rem` corners for tactile elegance.
- **Pill Badges (`9999px`)**: Category tags (e.g., "Titanium", "Progressive", "UV400") and frame size indicators use fully rounded pill profiles to contrast sharply against geometric cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid charcoal (`#1F2937`) background, `#FFFFFF` text, `0.5rem` radius, with subtle tactile feedback (`transform: translateY(-1px)` and background transition to `#111827`).
- **Accent**: Medical teal (`#0D9488`) background with white text, utilized strictly for optical checkouts, vision test bookings, and prescription confirmations.
- **Secondary / Ghost**: Pure `#FFFFFF` surface with `1px` `#E5E7EB` border, shifting to `#F1F3F5` on hover.

### Optical Cards & Product Tiles
- Crisp `#FFFFFF` surface framed with a 1px `#E5E7EB` border. Neutral padding (`space-lg`). High-definition eyewear visuals rest against transparent or soft `#F8F9FA` backdrops. Metadata displays frame name in `headline-sm`, followed by subtle material descriptors and lens types in `body-sm`.

### Chips & Badges
- Compact indicators rendered with a pill shape (`9999px`). Default state uses `#F1F3F5` background with `#374151` typography. Active filter chips toggle to a tinted teal shell (`#CCFBF1` background, `#0F766E` text, `1px solid #99F6E4`).

### Input Fields & Prescription Forms
- Form inputs feature a `44px` minimum height, `#FFFFFF` surface, `1px solid #E5E7EB` border, and `0.5rem` corner radius. Focused inputs transition crisply to `1px solid #0D9488` with a subtle teal glow ring (`0 0 0 3px rgba(13, 148, 136, 0.12)`). Numeric prescription inputs (SPH, CYL, AXIS) display monospaced alignments.

### Checkboxes & Radio Selectors
- Precision rounded squares for checkboxes (`4px` radius) and discs for radios. Inactive states feature `#E5E7EB` hairline outlines; selected states fill with `#0D9488` showing a centered white glyph.

### Drawers & Bottom Sheets
- Engineered for mobile ergonomics. Bottom sheets open over a `rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.3)` backdrop with smooth spring transitions, featuring a top drag affordance bar (`32px` width, `4px` height, `#E5E7EB` color) and `1rem` top radius corners for frame fitting customizers.
